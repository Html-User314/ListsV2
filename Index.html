    Backup and import

    Bringing back a file adds its lists. Lists you already have stay as they are. In a text file, each line becomes an item and lines starting with # become lists.

    Help

    The helper

    Click “Ask the helper” and say what you need in your own words, like “Add what I need for chicken soup to my shopping list” or “Make a packing list for a week at the beach”. You can also paste a whole recipe. The helper shows its suggestion first; untick anything you don’t want, then press the button to add it. It can also suggest ideas for a list, put a list in a sensible order, and remove duplicates.

    Adding things

    Type in the “Add an item” box at the bottom of a list, then press Enter or the Add button. To add many items at once, paste several lines.

    Make a new list with the New list button, or start from one of the ready-made lists on the main page. To put a list inside another one, open a list and choose “Add a list inside”.

    When you type in the Add box, things you’ve added to that list before are suggested, so you can add them again with one tap. If something is already on the list, it won’t be added twice.

    Finding your way

    Your lists are always shown on the left. On a phone or narrow window, tap Lists at the top to see them. Click a list to open it.

    To go back, use the Back button, click a name in the path above the list’s title, or use your browser’s back button.

    “Find a list or item” lets you type part of a name and go straight there.

    Changing things

    Click anywhere on an item to check it off, and click it again to uncheck it. To change its words, click its Edit button (the pencil), then press Enter or Done when you’re finished. Every list and item has an Options button (the three dots) with more choices. Right-clicking a list or item opens the same choices.

    To reorder items, drag the dotted handle beside each one, or use Move up and Move down in its options. Drop an item onto another list to move it there.

    Notes, dates and pins

    Click an item’s Edit button to change its words, add a note, or give it a date. Items with a date coming up appear under “Coming up” on the main page. Pin a list with its Pin button to keep it at the top. Archive a list from its Options to tuck it away without deleting it.

    Focus mode

    Focus shows a list big and simple, with large boxes to tap. It’s handy in a shop or when ticking things off one by one.

    Mistakes

    Undo puts back your last change. Use the Undo button, the Undo link that appears at the bottom of the screen, or press Ctrl Z.

    Text size and colors

    Open Text size at the bottom of your lists and pick one of the four sizes to make everything bigger or smaller. The + and − keys do the same.

    Settings lets you pick a color scheme, choose a light or dark background, and change the text size.

    Printing and saving

    Your lists save by themselves as you type. Print from the Print button on any list. You can print checked items as unchecked to get a fresh copy. Backup lets you download a copy and bring it back later.
